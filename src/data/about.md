# Currently

I am a Software Engineer passionate about Backend, Cloud and Distributed Systems. I hold a Master of Science in Computer Science Degree from Georgia Institute of Technology specializing in Computing Systems.

# Woven by Toyota Experience :
As a big believer in Autonomous and Software-Defined Vehicles, joining Woven by Toyota was a dream come true. I initially started in the ML Frameworks team, which provided the platform for model training, distributed data processing, and ETL workflows for autonomous driving. I built the [Distributed Processing Engine using Flyte Map tasks](https://flyte.org/blog/flyte-map-tasks-a-simpler-alternative-to-apache-spark) as a lightweight alternative to Apache Spark, which made debugging much easier for ML engineers. I also played a key role in advancing our multi-cloud training platform that supports thousands of training jobs with multi-million-dollar monthly infrastructure spend. I improved real-time observability so engineers could identify failures earlier, triage quicker, and avoid unnecessary cloud costs - directly supporting the faster delivery of our flagship autonomous driving models.
<br/><br/>
In 2023, I transitioned to the Arene team - Toyota's next-generation automotive software platform. I led the development of Vertex Studio, a test management and execution platform that validates vehicle software on thousands of virtual vehicles simultaneously. Think of it as stress-testing Toyota's software in thousands of simulated driving scenarios before it ever touches a real car. What started as a proof-of-concept became a production platform used by Toyota within 3 months, and it's planning to be deployed in actual RAV4 vehicles on the road!
<br/><br/>
Working in automotive software has been fascinating because it's where cutting-edge tech meets real-world safety. I've been fortunate to invent and file 6 patents (1 published, 5 pending) around vehicle software validation systems and autonomous vehicle safety - all assigned to Toyota. From building distributed processing engines to architecting large-scale vehicle validation platforms, I've gotten my hands dirty across the entire stack. The best part? Seeing the platform I helped build actually make vehicles safer for everyday drivers. It's a long way from debugging modem crashes at Samsung, but the thrill of solving complex systems problems remains the same!
